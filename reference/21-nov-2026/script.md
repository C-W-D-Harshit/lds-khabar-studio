# LDS खबर • 21 नवंबर 2026 • Full script and shot plan

Length 8:41 • 1920×1080 • 30 fps • Anchor (Lata) is voice-only, never on screen.

## Design system
- Frame: date block (top-left), navy headline strip, red category tag, yellow sub-strip, round LDS logo with rotating ring (top-right), content area 1840×750, yellow "एक नज़र में" ticker + red "ताज़ा ख़बरें" ticker at the bottom.
- Fonts: Khand (headlines), Mukta (body), Teko (numbers). Colours: navy #0B1F4D, red #E0141E, yellow #FFC915, green #16C76A.
- Every story background is its own blurred photo with a navy-red gradient, slow drifting light and grid. Film grain over everything.
- Story transitions: 1.3 s skewed-panel "bumper" wipes with category title (normal red/navy/yellow, fun yellow/red/purple with 🔥, somber grey with no sound).
- Somber story (S08) goes grayscale with no music and no SFX.
- Music: bed under voice at about 6–7% and swells in gaps; separate playful bed for the viral segment; music fades out for the tribute.
- Credits: every photo carries a "फोटो: source" chip; viral clips carry "वीडियो: X / handle".
